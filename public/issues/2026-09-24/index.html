01 · Games / industry

Xbox cuts 268 more jobs and hands the next Halo to Activision.

Microsoft says it is eliminating 268 positions across Halo Studios, other first-party teams and Xbox management, bringing its previously announced restructuring about three-quarters complete. Activision will now oversee Rare and World’s Edge and develop the next Halo with a new, separate team; Playground Games and Turn 10 are being combined around Forza and Fable.

Two attempted sales of Ninja Theory fell through, and Microsoft has begun consultation on a proposed closure. Double Fine, Compulsion Games and Undead Labs have already moved back to management ownership under divestiture arrangements.

Why it mattersFor game artists and technical teams, the significant part is the continued reduction of business units and jobs inside one of the world’s largest game publishers. Fewer internal studios and more franchise concentration can change where animation, cinematics, VFX and outsourced production work gets commissioned.

02 · Creative software / M&A

Adobe now owns Topaz Labs, while keeping its apps standalone.

Adobe completed its acquisition of Topaz Labs on September 23. Adobe says the Topaz brand, apps and models will remain available as standalone products, while Topaz CEO Eric Yang joins Adobe’s Digital Video and Audio team.

Topaz technology is already present in Firefly and Photoshop, and Adobe plans broader Creative Cloud integration for restoration, low-light footage, mobile clips, AI-video upscaling and other enhancement work. Topaz’s Neurostream technology can run complex models locally as well as through its cloud service.

Why it mattersFor VFX, editorial and finishing, deeper integration could remove some round-tripping between Adobe and Topaz tools. It also brings a prominent independent enhancement vendor inside Adobe; for now, Adobe’s stated commitment is that the standalone Topaz products will continue.

03 · Generative video

OpenAI shuts down the Sora 2 API today.

OpenAI’s Videos API, sora-2, sora-2-pro and their listed snapshots reach their shutdown date on September 24. The company announced the deprecation in March; its API deprecation table does not list a replacement for any of them.

The consumer Sora app and web experience were discontinued earlier this year, so today closes the remaining Sora 2 API route used by developers and automated pipelines.

Why it mattersIf a production tool depends on the Sora API, it now needs another video provider or a redesigned workflow. More broadly, it is a reminder to keep provider-specific generative-video calls isolated behind a replaceable layer when building production systems.

04 · AI audio / animation

Gemini 3.8 TTS adds prompt-designed voices and line-by-line dialogue direction.

Google released Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS with tools for designing voices from natural-language descriptions and directing individual lines for pacing, emotion, dialect shifts and conversational sounds. Flash is aimed at character and performance control; Flash-Lite targets higher-volume dubbing and voice-agent work.

The models are rolling out through the Gemini API and Google AI Studio, with Flash also appearing in Gemini Notebook and Flash-Lite in Google Vids. Google’s AI Studio workflow can also replicate a user’s own voice and place generated voices into a dual-speaker screenplay editor.

Why it mattersThis is directly useful for scratch dialogue, animatics, localization tests and interactive character prototypes. Imitating identifiable real voices still needs clear consent and rights handling; the practical gain here is faster control over a temporary or designed performance, not a substitute for those permissions.

05 · Open / local audio

NVIDIA releases a 100M open-weight model for “who spoke when.”

Nemotron 3 Diarization is a 100-million-parameter speaker-diarization model released September 23. It supports streaming and offline inference, handles up to eight speakers, can process recordings of unrestricted length through chunking and is licensed under OpenMDW 1.1 for commercial or non-commercial use.

NVIDIA provides a lightweight local C++ runtime through NeMo-Speech.cpp, and the same model can add speaker labels to an ASR transcription pipeline. Its lowest recommended streaming input buffer is 0.32 seconds.

Why it mattersLocal diarization is useful for interview logging, dailies, classroom critiques, podcasts and multi-speaker transcripts where audio should not be sent to a hosted service. The model identifies speaker turns rather than transcribing speech, so it is most useful paired with an ASR model.
