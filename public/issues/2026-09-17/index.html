01 · ComfyUI / local workflows

ComfyUI 0.36 makes loops a first-class workflow feature—and expands video, depth and audio support.

ComfyUI 0.36.0 landed late September 15 with Generic Loops, Video Concatenate, Marigold v2 depth support and Yue2 music generation. The release also includes MiniMax-H3 VAE memory optimizations plus new partner nodes for video editing, video generation and 3D workflows.

Why it mattersGeneric Loops is the headline feature for anyone building more procedural graphs: repeated processing can now live inside the workflow instead of being faked with duplicated nodes or external batch logic. The H3 VAE changes are also worth watching for heavier local video graphs, although custom-node-heavy setups should still be updated cautiously.

02 · VFX business / machine learning

Lightstorm Vision buys Outsyders, betting on machine-learning stereo conversion at production scale.

James Cameron’s Lightstorm Vision has acquired Outsyders, folding the company’s machine-learning stereoscopic conversion pipeline into Lightstorm’s native 3D capture and post-production system. Outsyders says its approach uses real and synthetic data to let small teams convert large volumes of footage; its recent work includes the 3D conversion of Lilo & Stitch, completed largely from 2D material in about eight weeks.

Why it mattersThis is a concrete example of AI being absorbed into a traditional VFX service rather than sold as a separate generative product. The interesting production question is not whether stereo conversion becomes “AI,” but whether smaller artist teams can now deliver high-end conversion work at a scale that changes budgets, schedules and staffing.

03 · Maya / animation

KeyFlow gives Maya animators 25+ free production tools plus a free shot-review app.

Animator Sebastian Jaksan has released KeyFlow, a free Maya toolkit covering tweening, pose libraries, animation transfer, transforms and baking, alongside KeyFlow Studio, a standalone A/B review and annotation application. KeyFlow Studio can manage reference footage, support collaborative reviews and send prepared reference back to Maya or Blender.

Why it mattersThis is the kind of small production tool that can have more day-to-day value than a flashy model release. It is free for commercial use, works with standard Maya rigs, and the review app could also be useful in classes where students need lightweight shot comparison and draw-over feedback without a full studio review system.

04 · AI productivity / teaching

Claude folds chat, Cowork, Docs, Slides and Design into one interface.

Anthropic is unifying Claude’s chat and Cowork experiences and adding Claude Docs and Claude Slides in beta, while bringing Claude Design into the same interface. The system is intended to choose the required capability automatically, with document exports to Google Docs or Word and presentation exports to PowerPoint or PDF.

Why it mattersThe important shift is away from choosing a separate “mode” for every task. For teaching, production planning and documentation, that means a single conversation can move from research to a handout, treatment, deck or visual mock-up without rebuilding context in another tool—a useful pattern to watch even if you use a different assistant.

05 · Local AI / Windows

Perplexity Portable Computer brings local AI agents to Windows RTX PCs.

Perplexity’s local agent platform is now available in its Windows app on compatible NVIDIA RTX hardware, running local models such as Qwen 3.8 27B for multistep work while keeping locally processed data on the machine. The app can also escalate to cloud models with permission and connects to services including Word, OneDrive, Google Drive, Gmail, Slack and GitHub.

Why it mattersThis is another sign that local AI is moving from “run a model in a chat box” toward full agent workflows that can operate across files and apps. The catch is hardware and lock-in: the Windows release currently targets NVIDIA RTX systems with at least 24GB of VRAM and requires a paid Perplexity plan, so it is more useful as a signal of where local agents are heading than as a universal solution.
