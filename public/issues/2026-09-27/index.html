01 · ComfyUI / video

ComfyUI adds native ID-V2V support for restyling filmed performances.

ComfyUI core merged ID-V2V support today. The model takes a source video plus a styled first frame or keyframes, then carries that look across the shot while trying to preserve the actor’s identity, gaze, expression and movement at 720p.

Why it mattersYou can record a performance first, then test a new look or lighting treatment without rebuilding the motion from scratch. The model is still a research release; the official setup is heavy, although single-GPU inference is supported with CPU offload.

02 · ComfyUI / color

ComfyUI’s color-space node now handles LogC3 and ACEScct.

ComfyUI core added LogC3 and ACEScct to its Convert Color Space node. A follow-up fix also corrected HDR output when converted images contain negative values.

Why it mattersThis is useful for VFX pipelines that need generated media to move through camera-log or ACES workflows. The changes are merged to core, so packaged releases may arrive later than the main branch.
Color-space commitHDR fix
03 · Blender / MCP

blend-ai 1.6 gives Blender agents real sub-object editing.

The open-source Blender MCP tool added vertex, edge and face selection, then let 19 mesh tools act on that selection instead of the whole object. Version 1.6 also adds path sweeps and Blender 3D Print Toolbox checks; a September 26 cleanup makes object-name parameters more consistent for agents.

Why it mattersCommands such as “bevel the top edge” can now map to a real selection-and-edit workflow. That is much closer to useful modeling assistance than whole-object operations.
GitHubv1.6 features
04 · 3DGS / local

Spirula Studio keeps tightening its local Gaussian-splat pipeline.

Fresh commits add Lang-SAM and BiRefNet masking, speed up structure-from-motion mapping and bundle adjustment, and reduce Vulkan device-loss failures on slower GPUs. The app already covers video or photos through splat training, editing, rendering and mesh export in one desktop tool.

Why it mattersIt is a practical local 3DGS option that runs through Vulkan on NVIDIA, AMD, Intel and Apple hardware, without a separate Python, PyTorch or COLMAP setup.
GitHubMasking update
05 · Capture / 3DGS

Superscenic turns an iPhone capture into a cloud-built Gaussian splat.

Superscenic launched September 25 as an iPhone app for capturing rooms, places and objects. Its 3DGS pipeline adds exposure normalization, depth and normal priors, and trained skyboxes before the result is shared and viewed online.

Why it mattersThis looks useful for fast location and reference capture. For now, treat it more as a capture and viewing tool than a full VFX asset pipeline; the launch post does not spell out a broad DCC export workflow.
